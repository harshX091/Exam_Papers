title: "Michelson Interferometer"
---

The connection lies in the fact that shifting the pattern from one bright ring to the very next (closest) bright ring represents a fringe count of exactly $N = 1$.

Here is the exact mathematical relationship between the two:

* **The Master Formula:** $2d = N\lambda$
* **Setting the Shift:** Since you want to find the mirror movement ($d$) required to shift the pattern between two closest bright rings, you are looking for exactly one fringe shift. Set $N = 1$.
* **The Calculation:**
$2d = (1)\lambda$
$d = \frac{\lambda}{2}$

**What this physically means:**
To make the interference pattern shift by exactly one bright ring, the movable mirror ($M_1$) only needs to move a physical distance of **half a wavelength** ($\frac{\lambda}{2}$).

Because the light must make a round trip, moving the mirror backward by $\frac{\lambda}{2}$ forces the light beam to travel an extra $\frac{\lambda}{2}$ forward and an extra $\frac{\lambda}{2}$ backward. This adds up to a total optical path difference of exactly $1\lambda$, which is the exact requirement to form the next bright ring in the sequence.
